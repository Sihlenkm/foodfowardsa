Our Programmes

Foodbanking

Foodbanking is our core model: recovering edible, in-date surplus food and non-food groceries from the supply chain and channelling it to registered, vetted beneficiary organisations. It is a cost-effective way to fight hunger while reducing food waste and its environmental impact.

How Foodbanking Works

  1. Recovery: surplus food is collected from farmers, manufacturers, and retailers before it goes to waste.
  2. Sorting and quality checks: food is inspected and sorted at one of our regional warehouses.
  3. Vetting beneficiary organisations: community organisations apply and are verified as registered non-profits.
  4. Distribution: beneficiary organisations collect food regularly from their nearest warehouse.
  5. Community feeding: partner organisations prepare and distribute meals directly to those in need.

Who We Serve

We currently support a network of more than 2,500 beneficiary organisations across all nine provinces, including soup kitchens, shelters, early childhood development centres, and community feeding schemes, prioritising organisations that also offer education, healthcare, or empowerment programmes alongside feeding.
