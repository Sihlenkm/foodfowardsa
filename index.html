Who We Are

Established in 2009 (formerly FoodBank SA), FoodForward SA is the largest food redistribution organisation in Africa. We recover edible, in-date surplus food from farmers, manufacturers, and retailers, and channel it to a national network of vetted beneficiary organisations — a model we call foodbanking.
